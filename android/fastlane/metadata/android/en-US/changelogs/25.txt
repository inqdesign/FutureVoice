New
• Ask your tutor. Tap the face at the bottom right of Talk and it looks through your talks, cards and notebook to answer questions about your practice. The buttons under an answer take you straight to that book or word.
• The tutor picks today's plan for you: one thing to do now and one next, with the rest folded away.
• Coming up: add an interview, exam, presentation, move or trip to your profile and your tutor prepares you for it every day. Attach the job posting or your CV and it writes the questions you'll likely get and a model answer, then practices them with you until they come out smoothly.
• When someone new joins the Core, you see their cube and can leave a heart or a cheer.
• In a Speech study script, press and hold any word to see what it means. Words you looked up stay marked.

Fixed

• The grammar focus only appears when the same slip comes back in that call.
• Your vocabulary level now reflects which words you use, not how many. It may read differently than before.
