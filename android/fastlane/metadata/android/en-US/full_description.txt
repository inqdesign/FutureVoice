Learn the language with the fluent you.

Your language isn't growing for one reason: you don't speak enough. And the real block isn't vocabulary. It's that pulling your own thoughts out of your head has never been trained. Not reciting memorized lines, but saying what you actually think, in your own words. That's where conversation starts. Speak, get your grammar and phrasing corrected, repeat daily. nawana is built for exactly that.

Sixty seconds of recording builds your fluent future self. Not a native speaker, not a voice from a movie. A goal you can actually reach: your own voice, fluent. Pitch, tone and pace stay yours. The only thing that changes is the language.

Practice English, German, Korean or Japanese. It's for people who can already put a sentence together, even haltingly.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Conversations start from your interests. One tap, like placing a call
2. Talk with the fluent future you. Stumble freely, every turn comes back fluent
3. Hang up, and your expressions, words and grammar are analyzed into your own textbook
4. Every word you say out loud checks itself off in your wordbook
5. Your level is measured only from what you actually said

TALK. It makes you speak, then finds what to fix
Call daily about free topics, situations you actually face, or news matched to your interests. Every session ends in a scorecard across five skills (vocabulary, grammar, expressiveness, fluency, pronunciation), and every fumbled line becomes a review card automatically.

WATCH. See tomorrow's conversation today
An interview, a doctor's visit, an awkward ask. Describe the situation in one line and your fluent self lives it first. Your lines play in your own fluent voice, and any sentence you want is one tap away from shadowing.

SHADOW. Until it comes out on its own
The voice you match is your own. Speak along with the fluent line, practice just the part that trips you up, and see a per-word verdict: nailed, swapped or skipped.

REVIEW. It returns right before you forget
Fumbled sentences come back as spaced-repetition drills, answered out loud, never multiple choice. CEFR-graded word lists sit underneath, and every word you use is ticked off automatically.

PROGRESS. Your level, from what you say
See your estimated CEFR level and how each skill is moving, measured from your real conversations.

A DAILY CALL
Pick a time and your fluent self calls you. Answer, and it opens with a question about your last talk.

YOUR VOICE, TREATED CAREFULLY
Recording is sixty seconds at setup, once. Nothing is ever recorded outside a conversation you started. Re-record or delete your voice anytime. Sessions, drills and progress stay on your phone.
