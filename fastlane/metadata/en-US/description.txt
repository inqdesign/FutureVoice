HOW IT WORKS

Conversations start from your interests, one tap, like placing a call
Talk with the fluent future you. Stumble freely, every turn comes back fluent
Hang up, and your expressions, words, and grammar are analyzed into your own textbook
Every word you say out loud checks itself off in your wordbook

TALK. It makes you speak, then finds what to fix
Call daily about free topics, situations you actually face, or news matched to your interests. Every session ends in a scorecard across five skills (vocabulary, grammar, expressiveness, fluency, pronunciation) and every fumbled line becomes a review card automatically.

WATCH. See tomorrow's conversation today
An interview, a doctor's visit, an awkward ask. Describe the situation in one line and your fluent self lives it first. Your lines play in your own fluent voice, and any sentence you want is one tap away from shadowing.

SHADOW. Until it comes out on its own
The voice you match is your own. Speak along with the fluent line, loop the hard part at 0.5x, and see a per-word verdict: nailed, swapped, or skipped.

REVIEW. It returns right before you forget
Fumbled sentences come back as spaced-repetition drills, answered out loud, never multiple choice. A pool of about 8,000 CEFR-graded words sits underneath, and every word you use is ticked off automatically.

YOUR VOICE, TREATED CAREFULLY
Recording is sixty seconds at setup, once. Nothing is ever recorded outside a conversation you started. Re-record or delete your voice anytime. Sessions, drills, and progress stay on your iPhone.
