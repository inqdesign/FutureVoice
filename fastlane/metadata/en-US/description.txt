Learn the language with the fluent you.

Your language isn't growing for one reason: you don't speak enough. And the real block isn't vocabulary — it's that pulling your own thoughts out of your head has never been trained. Not reciting memorized lines; saying what you actually think, in your own words. That's where conversation starts. Speak, get your grammar and phrasing corrected, repeat daily — nawana is built for exactly that.

Sixty seconds of recording builds your fluent future self. Not a native speaker, not a voice from a movie — a goal you can actually reach: your own voice, fluent. Pitch, tone, and pace stay yours. The only thing that changes is the language.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Conversations start from your interests — one tap, like placing a call
2. Talk with the fluent future you — stumble freely, every turn comes back fluent
3. Hang up, and your expressions, words, and grammar are analyzed into your own textbook
4. Every word you say out loud checks itself off in your wordbook
5. Your level is measured — only from what you actually said

TALK — it makes you speak, then finds what to fix
Call daily about free topics, situations you actually face, or news matched to your interests. Every session ends in a scorecard across five skills — vocabulary, grammar, expressiveness, fluency, pronunciation — and every fumbled line becomes a review card automatically.

WATCH — see tomorrow's conversation today
An interview, a doctor's visit, an awkward ask — describe the situation in one line and your fluent self lives it first. Your lines play in your own fluent voice, and any sentence you want is one tap away from shadowing.

SHADOW — until it comes out on its own
The voice you match is your own. Speak along with the fluent line, loop the hard part at 0.5×, and see a per-word verdict: nailed, swapped, or skipped.

REVIEW — it returns right before you forget
Fumbled sentences come back as spaced-repetition drills, answered out loud — never multiple choice. A pool of ~8,000 CEFR-graded words sits underneath, and every word you use is ticked off automatically.
