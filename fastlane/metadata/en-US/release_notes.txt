New
• Coach mode: now and then your future self asks something you can answer with a word you're studying, and shows that word as a hint while you think. Turn it on in Call settings.
• People sound like themselves. A friend talks like a friend, a public figure like themselves. When you add someone, you can choose how casually the two of you talk.
• When you're learning Korean or Japanese, your conversation partners speak with voices and names from that language.
• The app is now available in German.
