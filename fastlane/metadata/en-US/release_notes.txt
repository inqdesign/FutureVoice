New
• Speech: a new tab for practicing talks and presentations. Put your script on screen and read it out loud while it moves along with your voice. Turn on the camera to record a video with your script and your face in one frame. When you finish, you get a score for sticking to the script, your pace and your pauses.
• Week in review: when a week ends, flip through last week's calls and the words and expressions you picked up, with this week's test on the last card.
• Grammar focus: pick one mistake you keep making and keep it in mind for the whole call. You'll see it right away if it comes up again. On by default at A1 and A2.
• When you first meet your voice, you can hear and choose from four accents right there.
• You can now learn the language you use the app in.
• Talk has a new Everyday tab: everyday situations like a café, a restaurant, a clothing store or calling a clinic, one tap to start.
• Each tab now opens with a short guide the first time: what it's for and how to use it. See them again anytime in Me → App guide.
• My routine: plan your week right on the timetable and drag blocks to move them. Put talks, reviews and Say it again on the days and times you want; the daily call rings at your routine's talk times.
• Export a book as a PDF and you get a workbook you can start filling in right away.
• Questions you missed during the month keep coming back in your weekly test until you get them right.
