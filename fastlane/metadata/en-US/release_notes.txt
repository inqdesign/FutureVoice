New
• Tutor mode is new. Pick a topic and your future self teaches it in 3 to 4 steps, practicing each one with you until you say it without a mistake. A checklist above the call shows where you are, and a lesson you stop picks up on your next call.
• When a lesson is done, choose to keep going on the same topic or move to another.
• Tap the book next to a Podcast episode to turn it into your own review book.
• Before your first call, you can set your level, speed, Coach mode, Answer with Send and Tutor mode.
• When you first make your voice, you can pick a voice that sounds like yours instead of your own.
• Pick as many answers to "Why are you learning?" as you like.
• The Voice page in Settings is simpler and easier to scan.
• The Speech tab now shows up to 10 challenges.

Fixed
• Noise no longer plays before the first line of a Watch scene.
• Your bubble no longer disappears and comes back after you finish speaking in a call.
• The Shadowing countdown no longer hangs on "3".
• The timer in a call and the ring on Home now show the same talk time.
• In the weekly test, fixing just the underlined part now counts as a right answer, and translation questions now mean the same thing as their answers.
• Languages without Podcast episodes no longer show an empty Podcast tab.
• Your public intro preview no longer opens every time you go to Watch.
• Taking your profile down now takes it down in every language you published it in.
