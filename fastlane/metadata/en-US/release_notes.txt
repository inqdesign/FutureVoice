NEW · Find people
Practice with someone you've never met, like meeting a stranger at a language school. Open Watch → Find people:
• People — other learners who published an introduction
• Characters — a surf hostel owner two months into a decision she keeps avoiding, a game tester who found a bug nobody wants fixed, a night-shift controller who just turned down the day shift and doesn't know why. Everyone here is in the middle of something.
Talk with anyone live, or watch your fluent self talk with them first. Every talk becomes a book in Practice, with its own words and lines to master. Bookmark the ones you want to keep — they join your People row.
Want others to practice with "you"? Me → Find people writes your introduction into the pool.

NEW · Daily call
Your fluent self can call you at a time you pick. Answer and you're straight into a conversation; miss it and there's a voicemail waiting.
