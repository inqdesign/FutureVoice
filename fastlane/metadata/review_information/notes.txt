ABOUT THE VOICE CLONE (important for review):
- During onboarding the user records a 60-second sample of their OWN voice, with explicit consent, to create a personal text-to-speech voice (via ElevenLabs).
- The app can only clone the signed-in user's own voice — there is no way to import audio of another person or clone anyone else.
- The clone is used solely to speak the user's practice lines back in their own voice ("your fluent self"). Re-recording permanently deletes the previous clone; the user can delete their voice or entire account in-app (Me tab).
- Consent is a separate, recorded step shown before the microphone is ever requested (age 16+ and voice-model consent are two distinct toggles), because a voice model is biometric data under GDPR Art. 9, Illinois BIPA, and Korea's PIPA.

REVIEW LOGISTICS:
- "Get started" begins onboarding WITHOUT an account (anonymous session). The voice clone is built first; Sign in with Apple is asked right after, to keep the voice — any Apple ID works. Sign in with Apple is the only auth method.
- Onboarding requires microphone + speech recognition permissions and the 60-second recording before the main app unlocks.
- All AI requests (Gemini, ElevenLabs) route through our Supabase Edge Functions; no provider API keys ship in the binary.
- Subscriptions are auto-renewable and are metered per BILLING PERIOD, not per day — there is no daily cap on either tier, and a subscriber may spend a whole period in one sitting. Light buys a pool of 150 talk minutes. Plus places NO limit on talk time at all: the server enforces no talk ceiling for that tier, and its plan card reads "Talking — No limit" with no figure, because there is no figure to print. Watch scenes ARE capped on BOTH tiers and the exact count is printed on both cards (60 a period on Light, 120 on Plus). The two sides differ because a Watch scene plays itself on a tap, whereas talking requires the user to actually speak — effort bounds the one, so only the other needs a count. Each card states all of this as four labelled rows: the talk row, the Watch row, and two rows reading "Unlimited" for the personal review book and for shadowing/words/replays/drills, none of which are ever metered. A subscriber who reaches a cap that does apply to them is shown a sheet stating it and pointing at the free review — never a paywall, since they have already paid. A 7-day free trial is metered at a pro-rated share of the Light pool, so a trial is never uncapped. Restore and cancel via the App Store. There is no free tier: the voice clone and the first spoken greeting are free, and the first conversation reaches the paywall.
- PLEASE NOTE, in case the product identifiers look inconsistent: the two tiers are named Light and Plus everywhere the buyer sees them (paywall, settings, localized display names), but their Apple product ids still read "…daily_monthly", "…daily_annual", "…unlimited_monthly", "…unlimited_annual". Those ids were registered before the tiers were renamed, and an Apple product id cannot be renamed or reused. The ids are internal only. No tier is NAMED "Unlimited"; the word appears on a plan card only against the review and practice features, which genuinely are unmetered, and every cap that does apply to a tier is printed on its card.
- Reviewing, replaying, and building situations never consume talk time, so the app remains fully usable after the monthly allowance is spent. Running out shows a sheet that points to the free review, never a dead end.
