- Sign in with Apple is the only auth method and any Apple ID works, so there is no demo account to provide. Onboarding requires microphone and speech recognition permission and a 60-second recording before the main app unlocks.

- That recording creates a personal text-to-speech voice (via ElevenLabs). The app can only clone the signed-in user's own voice; there is no way to import audio of another person. Consent is a separate screen shown before the microphone is requested. The voice or the entire account can be deleted in-app (Me tab).

- There is no free tier: the voice clone and the first spoken greeting are free, and the first conversation reaches the paywall.

- The subscription product ids read "...daily_monthly", "...daily_annual", "...unlimited_monthly" and "...unlimited_annual" because they were registered before the tiers were renamed to Light and Plus, and an Apple product id cannot be renamed or reused. No tier is NAMED "Unlimited", and each plan card prints the caps that apply to it.
