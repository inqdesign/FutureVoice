# 티저 영상 — copy (v1)

One vertical video, 1080×1920, 38 s. These are the text beats in the brief's timeline order.
Each beat was written in Korean from the brief's intent first, then written again in English.
They aren't translations of each other. Korean renders first.

Sound (lead default): `2026-10-11-what-you-can-do/audio/3-clone.mp3` plays at 0.4 s, on the
opening pill only. No music, and nothing plays under the `nw-call` beats.

Layout: one text beat on top in the pixel face, the `nw-phone` below. Conversation lines in the
call stay English in both versions, as the kit draws them. Labels around them follow the
version.

Length rule: montage beats are on screen ~1.4–1.8 s, so each Korean beat there is 2–4 words
(≤ 13 Hangul). The longest Korean beat is 5 words (08, 2.0 s). No beat needs a line break
except where marked `\n`.
