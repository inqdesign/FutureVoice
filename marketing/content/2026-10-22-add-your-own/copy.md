Series: 나와나로 할 수 있는 것
Written 2026-10-22 from `brief.md`. Korean written as Korean, English as English; they agree in
meaning, not wording. 해요체 throughout, no 당신, no em dash anywhere in this file.

The one message: 밖에서 주운 단어·표현·문장도 목록에 직접 넣으면, 통화에서 나온 것과 똑같이 공부가 돼요.
The using loop, never "wordlist 외우기". The CEFR level is the WORD's, not the learner's. "Shared"
is a warm feature (term + level + language only, after the check passes), never a privacy ask.

## For the designer

- **Kit = `nw-words` only** (the leveled word cloud + the word card). The add-input and
  "Already there" moments have no capture — carry them through the cloud + a faithful add-sheet
  sticker, or a new faithful pose on `nw-words`. **Do NOT draw a new screen.** (Kit gaps 1–2, per brief.)
- **Hero word: "leverage"** (designer confirms against the kit cloud sample). Level badge on card 04
  is kit sample data (e.g. B2), never a user stat.
- Every number/level/count on a slide is kit sample data. No title or desc restates them.
- **The voice is the learner's own clone** (card 05), not a native or tutor voice. Keep e13/e14 consistency.
- App labels stay the app's own (단어장 / Words, '+', 뜻 / meaning, 알아요 / Known, etc.).

## Instagram caption (en)

You collect words out in the wild, write them down to memorize, and never do.

Drop that word straight onto your nawana list instead.

Tap "+" on your Words list and type what you met, in either language.
As you type, anything already there pops up, so you never make a duplicate.
A word the library doesn't have yet is checked once and gets a CEFR level, then joins the one library everyone learning English shares. Your word helps the next learner too. (Typos and made-up words get turned away.)
After that it's just like a word your fluent self handed you: hear it in your own voice, and it's in today's deck, on your home-screen widget, in review.

Tap the word and it reads aloud in your own voice.
