Other store moves, in order

  1. Change the promotional text now.no review

    It is the one field that updates without a new version. Every proposal above opens with "first 20 minutes free", where the live text opens on "your language isn't growing".

  2. Ship the screenshots, name and subtitle with 1.2.4.next version

    Screenshots belong to a version and the app info only unlocks while one is being prepared, so this rides on the next submission. asc-submit.py writes text only; screenshots need an upload step (I can add one once you pick the sets).

  3. A/B test the first screenshot in Korea and the US.Product Page Optimization

    Live brand hero against the new "talk with the fluent you" call screen. Up to three treatments, 90 days, needs no app update.

  4. Custom product pages for the biggest intents.custom product pages

    "영어 면접", "오픽 / 토익스피킹", "Job interview in English", "英会話が恥ずかしい". Each gets its own URL to use in Instagram and Threads links and Apple Search Ads, with the situation or speech shot first.

  5. Record a real App Preview.15–30 s

    A simulator screen recording of one call: the question, the learner's line, the correction card, the fluent self's answer in the same voice. Sound matters here because the voice is the product.

  6. Run the weekly speech challenge as an In-App Event.search + Today

    Events show in search results and on the product page, so a new challenge each week is a recurring reason to appear.

  7. Make an iPad 13-inch set.still old

    The app runs on iPad, and the seven live iPad shots are as old as the iPhone ones. They need a pass on the iPad simulator; reusing iPhone frames on an iPad page is a rejection risk.

Before any of this goes live

  • Headlines in ko, ja, de, fr, es, zh-Hant and zh-Hans were written for each market, not translated. A native read is still worth it for ja, de, fr, es and both Chinese sets before upload.
  • Screens use the capture harness's sample data: sample names, an English talk about a job interview, a B2 level. Two leftovers show in every language: the correction card's grammar label reads "Past tense", and the routine list repeats one sample talk several times.
  • Keyword indexing across locales (for example es-MX counting in the US store) comes from ASO tools, not Apple documentation. Check it against Search Ads data before relying on it.
  • The name changes are the strongest ranking lever and the most visible change. They are proposals; the current names stay until you decide.
