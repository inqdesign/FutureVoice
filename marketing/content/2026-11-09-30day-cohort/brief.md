# Brief: 30일 기수 프로모션 캐러셀 (2026-11-09-30day-cohort)

- stage: brief → copy
- piece: 7 cards, 1080×1350, Korean and English. Card 1 is a promotion poster, not a cover.
- offer and claim limits: fixed by the founder and the billing check. Not to be re-argued in
  copy. No em dashes in anything published.

## One message

**이번엔 끝까지: [모집 마감일]까지 구독하고 30일 중 20일, 유창해진 내 목소리와 말하면 한 달이
무료예요.** (완주 후 다음 결제 1회 무료)

EN intent: this time you finish. Join by the deadline, speak on 20 of the 30 days with your
fluent self, and a month is free (your next payment after finishing, once).

Locked claim, Korean: "30일 완주하면 한 달 무료" with fine print "완주 후 다음 결제 1회 무료".
English says the same thing natively, not as a translation of the Korean. It is not a discount
sale; the reason to act is finishing, the free month is the reward.

## Who it's for

Someone who has the app, cloned their voice, tried a call or two and stopped without
subscribing. They liked it and it slid. Plus followers who have been watching. The moment:
seeing a date that closes and thinking "그럼 이번엔 해 볼까". Honest floor as always: someone
who can build a sentence, stumbling. No zero to fluent.

## Why now

A limited recruiting window of 7 days that really closes. The window is the only "now" this
piece has, so the deadline appears on the poster and the last card.

### What makes card 1 a poster

- Breaks the fixed layout at the founder's request: no series label line, no n/total, no phone,
  no 2-line description block.
- **One huge thing:** "30일" in the pixel face, filling most of the card width. Nothing else
  competes with it.
- **The offer at feed size:** "완주하면 한 달 무료" is the second-largest element and reads on a
  grid thumbnail.
- **The deadline is its own block:** "[모집 마감일] 마감", high contrast, not buried in small
  type.
- **The condition is visible, not implied:** one line, about 32 px or larger at 1080 wide:
  기간 내 구독 · 30일 중 20일 말하기 완주 시 · 다음 결제 1회 무료 · 이후 정상가 자동 갱신.
- Ground and motif are the founder's call, so show two: (A) the card flooded in accent #0A5CF5
  with white numerals, (B) the light grid ground with numerals in ink and the Futureself mosaic
  clipped inside the digits. Both are new to this team's visuals. Still PNG, must work in the
  grid. Community slides are light renders only, so (A) needs an exception or a light variant.

### Card 6, the terms (intent, copywriter words it)

- 대상: [모집 마감일]까지 구독한 사람 (plan and platform scope: open questions 2 and 3)
- 조건: [기수 시작일]부터 30일 중 20일, 하루 [하루 기준] 이상 말하기
- 혜택: 완주 후 다음 결제 1회 무료
- 이후: 정상가로 자동 갱신, 언제든 해지 가능 (Apple's subscription settings)

This card exists so the condition is unmissable. It is why this card has no phone.

## Screens: what they can and can't prove

- **There is no cohort screen in the app.** No join screen, no 20/30 counter, no list of
  cohort members. Never draw one, and don't dress an existing screen as one.
- `nw-routine` shows the learner's own promise streak ("N일째 지키는 중"), which would read as a
  cohort counter. Use the day list with that line out of frame or kept generic, else use `nw-ring`.
- Don't use the Core (seal, indigo, its streak). It is a different thing, 30 days in a row.
- Kit numbers are sample data. Never present them as results.

## Channels

IG carousel (card 1 and 7 PNG, cards 2 to 5 as 8 to 12 s loops), Threads (shorter, conversational,
keeps the condition). Community: a dated news post rather than a feature-series entry, because
the offer expires and the series is a permanent archive. The people who already have the app are
better reached in the app or by push and mail; that is outside this piece and the lead's call.

## Risks and claim limits

**Banned wording:** 환급, 100%, 공짜로 배우기, 0원, 둘째 달 무료, and English equivalents (refund,
$0, learn for free, second month free). Korea's FTC has sanctioned this wording in education ads.

1. **Not verified, blocks publishing (status.md):** when an Apple free code applies, and a code
   arriving as `FREE_TRIAL` would be filed as `trialing` and cut that month's talk pool to 7/30.
   "다음 결제 1회 무료" should not go out until both are settled.
2. **Placeholders stay visible until decided:** `[모집 마감일]`, `[기수 시작일]`, `[하루 기준]`. The
   4 minutes is provisional and must not be printed.
3. **No price.** BRAND.md (Plus 600 min, Max 1,200) disagrees with CLAUDE.md (Plus 450, Max 600 for
   new buyers), so it can't be treated as current. Say "정상가", no figure. Lead should fix BRAND.md.
4. **Renewal is said out loud** on cards 1, 6 and 7.
5. **"함께" must stay true.** The app shows the learner nothing of other cohort members. Say they
   start the same day, and no more: no leaderboard, no group, no number joined, no seat limit.
6. **The deadline must be real.** Extending it after "마감" makes the ad itself misleading.
7. **No statistics in copy.** "대부분 하루 만에 멈춰요" is an internal reason, and printing it is a
   number claim as well as a negative opener. BRAND: positive first.
8. **Not a trial.** BRAND: there is no free trial. Never call the free month 체험.
9. **Outro line.** BRAND's fixed outro has "첫 20분은 무료예요". Recommend dropping it here: this
   audience has used those minutes, and a second "무료" muddles the conditional one. Lead to confirm.
10. Android: no claim either way. No testimonials.

## Open questions for the founder

1. How does someone join: is subscribing in the window enough, or is there a separate step? What
   does the CTA point at (paywall deep link, nawana.app)?
2. Who qualifies: new subscribers only, or current ones too? Which plans; does annual count?
3. Platform scope: iPhone only in the fine print until Android's reward path is settled?
4. How does a learner see their count of 20? Without a surface, the promise can't be checked by
   the person making the effort, and card 4 has nothing true to show for it.
5. What counts as a day: a call only, or any speaking (say it again, shadowing, speech)?
6. Poster: ground A or B, and is a poster exception to the light-only community rule fine?

## Success signal

Saves and shares on the poster, profile visits, link taps, subscriptions started inside the window
from this audience. Afterwards: share of joiners who finish.
