# Pilot: persona channel, episode 1 (long-form, 16:9, about 6 minutes)

Not for posting. A sample so the founder can judge the format.

**Channel idea (founder):** one YouTube channel, a cast of fictional people who each need English for real (a student going abroad, a flight attendant applicant, a nurse going abroad, a store clerk), each with a playlist that follows their whole journey. Viewers find the person whose life looks like theirs and practice along.

**This episode:** Harin, 28, fictional. Three years on the marketing team of a company in Seoul, admitted to a master's in business in the US. No school or company is named. Today is her student visa interview at the embassy in Seoul. The hard moment: the officer asks how he can know she'll come back. Scene is in `scene.json` (final, English).

**Audience:** Korean speakers who can build a sentence, stumbling. They'd find this by searching for visa interview English, or because the next step of their own life looks like hers.

**Shape:** cold open on the hard question → who Harin is → watch the whole scene with subtitles → 3 expressions → repeat her 3 hard answers (silent gaps, no need to touch the phone) → your turn: officer speaks, viewer answers in the gap → watch again → close.

**Must hold:** openly fictional, an example conversation; real interview questions differ per person; no claim that any answer gets a visa approved; no claim she used the app or that the app helped anyone. The app appears only at the close: practicing a situation of your own, in your own voice. Honest about Watch: a learner can type their own upcoming situation, watch it played, and shadow the lines.
