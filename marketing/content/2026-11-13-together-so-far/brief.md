# Brief: together so far (2026-11-13-together-so-far)

- stage: brief → copy
- piece: ONE carousel, 8 cards, 1080×1350 (4:5). Korean and English, each written natively
  by the copywriter from the intent below (no sentence here is source text to translate).
- source of truth: `data.md` in this folder (every number, the founder's rule, the "Do not
  use" list), `status.md` (the founder's words), BRAND.md, CHANNELS.md, CLAUDE.md.
- This is NOT a feature piece and NOT an acquisition post. It sits outside the
  "나와나로 할 수 있는 것" series.

## One message

**혼자 연습하는 게 아니에요. 같은 시간에, 다른 나라에서, 같은 걸 하는 사람들이 있어요. 함께해 줘서 고마워요.**

EN intent: you are not practising alone. Other people are doing the same thing, at the same
hours, in other countries. Thank you for the time and the trust.

Every card answers one question a current user has never been able to ask: "who else is
here?" The thanks comes last and is said once.

## Who it's for

Someone who already uses nawana. Their moment: they take a call before work or late at
night, alone in a room, talking to their own voice. Nothing in the app shows them another
learner (by design: talks stay on the phone). They have no idea whether ten people or ten
thousand do this. This piece shows them the room without showing anyone in it.

Not written for a stranger. If a non-user sees it, fine, but no card explains the app, sells
a plan or asks for a download.

## Why now

- The founder asked for it (status.md).
- There are two and a half months of metered calls on file (2026-09-01 to 2026-11-13),
  enough for the shares to be stable.
- The app got louder this month (Android, new languages, creators). A piece that only says
  thank you and asks for nothing balances that.

## The number rules (repeat of the founder's rule, binding for copy and design)

- Whole-community TOTALS are real numbers: 85 hours, about 2,000 calls, more than 14,000
  turns, 24 countries.
- Everything else is a percentage or a plain-word share.
- Never a count of people (users, callers, subscribers, signups, voices). Never anything
  about one person. No medians.
- The ~750 h "time in app" figure is NOT used anywhere in this piece (an estimate that
  includes onboarding; `data.md`).
- The window is said honestly: "9월부터 지금까지" / "since September". Not "since launch".
- Round the way `data.md` says: "85 hours", "about 2,000", "more than 14,000".

## Facts picked (7) and facts left out

Picked: 85 call hours · 14,000+ turns over about 2,000 calls · 24 countries with the three
biggest shares · the two daily peaks (before work, before bed) seen in both Korea and
Germany · weekdays 80%, Monday busiest · languages by call time · what people do beyond the
call.

Left out, on purpose:
- "47% came back on another day": to a current user it reads as "half left". It makes the
  room smaller, which is the opposite of the message.
- "October was 2.8x September": a growth figure is for investors. Held in reserve; if the
  founder wants a "the room is getting bigger" beat, it can replace card 5.
- More than 1,200 shadowing takes: a second total would compete with the call numbers.
  Card 7 carries shadowing as a share instead.
- ~750 h in app: see above.

### Closing card (8)

- Says three things, in this order: thank you for the time; thank you for the trust (they
  gave the app their own voice); see you on the next call. Each short. No rationale.
- Speaker is nawana ("we"), in 해요체 for Korean. No founder name, no signature with a
  person's name (BRAND.md: no real names in public material).
- Not the fluent self speaking. If the copywriter wants a fluent-self line anywhere, it is
  informal and never longing ("보고 싶었어" and the like are out).
- No call to action beyond the app they already have. The standard outro lines
  ("첫 20분은 무료예요" and the tagline) are acquisition lines; this brief proposes
  dropping "첫 20분은 무료예요" here and keeping wordmark + nawana.app. Founder to confirm
  (open question 2).

## Risks (for the copywriter, designer and reviewer)

1. **BRAND.md says numbers on a slide are illustrative sample data, never user statistics.**
   This piece is the deliberate exception: its numbers ARE real community statistics from
   `data.md`. The reviewer checks every figure against `data.md`, digit by digit, and
   nothing else may appear.
2. **Sample numbers next to real ones.** A kit screen shows sample scores and minutes. On a
   card that also carries a real statistic, a reader will take the sample for real. So only
   card 7 has a phone, and its `nw-shadow` state should show as few figures as possible
   (designer picks the state; reviewer confirms nothing on it reads as a community stat).
3. **The time-of-day claim is measured for Korea and Germany only.** Say "in Korea and in
   Germany", or "the two largest groups". Not "everywhere", not "all 24 countries". "About
   half" is the only quantity; the German peaks are named as hours, with no percentage.
4. **No invented chart shapes.** Card 4 has no hourly data behind it; card 3's "rest" is
   one segment (about 26%), not 21 separate slices.
5. **"85 hours" framing.** Never "only", never compared with anything, never divided per
   person or per day (that would produce an average and expose the size of the community).
   Same for calls and turns: no "per learner".
6. **Percentages of people can leak a count if two are combined with a total.** Don't put
   "24 countries" and a per-country headcount-like phrase together ("a handful in…",
   "one learner in…"). Shares only.
7. **Turns are not sentences.** "More than 14,000 turns" counts only calls that reported a
   session record, hence "more than". Don't call them sentences or words.
8. **French and Chinese** were added to the code on 2026-11-13 and have no data. Don't
   mention them on card 6 unless the founder confirms they are in the store build on the
   day this posts (open question 4).
9. **Korean 1% is "Korean as the language practised"**, while Korea 43% is where learners
   are. Cards 3 and 6 must not blur the two.
10. **Tone traps:** no "우리 커뮤니티가 이만큼 성장했어요" (growth talk), no hype words, no
    grading ("열심히 하신 분들"), no guilt for weekend or lapsed users, no em dash in any
    copy, no emoji decoration, never "당신".
11. **Public channel, private audience.** Posted on Instagram, the totals are visible to
    anyone, including people who will judge the app's size by them (open question 1).

## Success signal

This piece is not judged by reach. Look at, one week after posting:
- replies, comments and DMs from existing users (the real measure: did anyone answer back);
- shares to stories and sends (belonging gets passed on);
- community news views if it is posted there;
- saves and profile visits are secondary here.
