## Open questions for the lead

1. **Card 5 arithmetic.** RESOLVED by the lead (2026-11-13): the "80%" is out of the
   description and said in words; the seven rounded bars stand alone.
2. **Card 2, "across about 2,000 calls".** `data.md` says the 14,000 turns come only from
   calls that reported a session record, so they are from a subset of the 2,000 calls. The
   copy says the turns were taken across about 2,000 calls with "more than" / "넘게" on the
   turns, as the brief does. If the reviewer wants it stricter, the two numbers can stand
   as separate lines with no "across".
3. **Card 3, what the percentages measure.** Korea 43% etc. are shares of PEOPLE who
   called, not of calls. The title says "calling from 26 countries" and the bar is captioned
   "Where learners are" so the bar is not read as call share. The percentages sit on the
   bar only, not in the description.
4. **Card 4.** No number on the card at all. "About half before 9 am or after 8 pm" is a
   Korea-only figure, so I left it out instead of attaching it to a line that names both
   countries. "출근 전 / before work" is the wording `data.md` asks for; the data is clock
   time, not anyone's job.
5. **Card 7, pronunciation.** RESOLVED by the lead (2026-11-13): the pronunciation 12% is
   removed. It counts takes scored in the background, not a feature someone chose. The card
   keeps four, in the app's own names: 섀도잉, 상황연습, 튜터, 다시 말하기.
6. **Card 7 tense.** The shares are "people who called and also did X at least once" over
   the window, so the copy says "해 봤어요 / have tried", not a habit.
7. **Card 6.** French and Chinese are left out (no data, added today).
8. **"함께 / together" on the cover.** It means added together across everyone, not people
   in one call. The description says "모두 더했어요 / added together" to keep that clear.
9. **Group chat message register.** Two Korean versions are given (합니다체 and 해요체);
   the founder picks. It thanks once; the cards thank once, on card 8.
10. **Hashtags** are a guess at the account's usual set; there is no handle on file in
    CHANNELS.md.
