The one message: 자유 대화는 내가 끌어가요 — 하고 싶은 걸 말로 부탁하면 그대로 해줘요. Not a fixed
lesson (that's tutor mode, e06), not the app volunteering help (that's corrections e04 /
coach-suggestion e21). This is the opposite: **YOU ask, it does.**

Korean written as Korean, English written as English — not translations of each other. On-screen
conversation lines (`say()` / `user()`) are MATERIAL and stay **English on both versions**. The
fluent self is casual. No pronunciation claims anywhere (the reply gets text, not audio — keep
requests to expressions, mistakes, explanations, opinions, a part to play). No `fix(...)` and no
`coach(...)` card on any screen: the teaching and the correction both arrive as the fluent self's
spoken reply to your request. `call.other` stays UNSET (free talk, the fluent self).
