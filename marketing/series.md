## Proposed order 03–06 (strategist, 2026-10-13)

Founder's earlier order was core → Speech → Watch → Routine. Shadowing took slot 02, so the
rest keeps the founder's sequence and the story keeps growing by one size each time:
one line (02) → a whole script (03) → a situation before it happens (04) → every day (05) →
one real week that uses all of it (06). Every piece stays on the same interview story.

- **03 · Speech: from one line to a whole script.** Read your own script (the interview
  self-introduction) on a prompter, get scored on the same honest read as shadowing. Comes right
  after 02 because it is the same act, scaled up, and the thread reads as a progression.
  Ends on the score; challenges at most as the last card.
- **04 · Watch: see the interview before you walk in.** Describe the real situation (attach the
  posting), watch your fluent self handle it, keep its lines. Story order: the call in 01 was
  ABOUT an interview, Watch is how you prepare for the next one. Renders in
  `2026-10-11-what-you-can-do/renders-v3/05` are a head start.
- **05 · Routine: it calls you, every day.** Daily call (rings through silent mode), the routine
  as your own promise, the streak, the weekly report + weekly test from your own week. After
  three "what it does" pieces, this one is "how it sticks".
- **06 · Interview week (capstone, backlog M).** Mon Watch → call → book → Say it again →
  daily call → weekend test, one learner's week. Say it again (the whole talk said again,
  the right way) lands here rather than in its own piece: it needs the call and the book to
  make sense, and 02 already owns "say it after your fluent self". If the founder wants Say it
  again standalone, it slots between 05 and 06.
