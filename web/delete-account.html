Delete your nawana account

nawana · Dear RoRo, Munich, Germany

In the app

Open Me → Delete account and confirm. It works the same on Android and iPhone, and it takes effect immediately.

Without the app

Email hello.dearroroapp@gmail.com from the address you signed in with (Google or Apple), with the subject Delete my nawana account.

We confirm by reply and delete the account within 30 days, usually the same day.

What is deleted

  • Your voice clone, here and at ElevenLabs, and the recording it was made from.
  • Your account: sign-in, profile, settings, talk-time balance, invite codes, your Find people listing, and usage records tied to the account.
  • A web subscription bought at nawana.app is cancelled at once.

What is kept

  • Payment records are unlinked from you but kept for as long as tax law requires: amount, date and store transaction id.
  • Usage events in our analytics (for example "a talk ended") stay under a random id that no longer points to any account.
  • Your practice data on the phone (talks, cards, words, progress) was never on our servers. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Subscriptions from a store

Deleting the account does not stop a subscription billed by Google Play or Apple. Cancel it there: Google Play → Subscriptions or Apple → Subscriptions.

Only your voice

To delete your voice and keep your account: Me → Privacy → Withdraw consent & delete my voice.
