Why your own voice

A native speaker's voice was never yours — you can imitate it forever and never arrive. Your fluent clone keeps your timbre, so the only distance left between you and the goal is fluency itself.

01Talk · Output

Speaking practice that writes your curriculum.

You only learn to speak by speaking — and the exact places you stall are a syllabus no textbook can see. Talk makes you produce, then catches what to fix.

  • Any topic, anytime. Free talk, a situation you choose, or news matched to your interests. Every conversation is saved to your record.
  • Every turn, a fluent rewrite. Your sentence comes back the way your fluent self would say it — same meaning, better English.
  • Sessions end in a scorecard. Five axes, graded in code — and every fumbled line becomes a drill card automatically.

02Watch · Input

You can't say what you've never heard.

The scariest part of a foreign language is the blank — not knowing what to say next. In Watch, your fluent self carries that exact conversation with the real people from your life, and you hear how you'd put it.

  • Partners you created. A colleague, your landlord, the pediatrician — described once, played with real context.
  • Live every situation in advance. The negotiation, the small talk, the awkward ask — a full dialogue, your lines in your fluent voice.
  • Then shadow it into yours. Any line that sticks in your ear — one tap and you're speaking it until it's really yours.

03Practice · Imprint

Shadow it until it's automatic.

Understanding a phrase isn't owning it — your mouth needs the reps. Shadowing imprints pronunciation, rhythm, and intonation in one motion, and here the target is a voice you can actually match: yours.

  • Sync shadowing. The fluent line plays while you speak along — afterward, every word shows whether you nailed it, swapped it, or skipped it.
  • Loop the hard part. Tap the words that trip you, slow to 0.5×, repeat — with a deterministic match score.
  • Spaced repetition. Your fumbled sentences return as speak-aloud drills right before you'd forget them.

Words · Vocabulary

Every word you speak joins your world.

The words from your conversations gather into one cloud. Drag through it, tap any word for its meaning and examples. Studying words carry a bookmark, known ones a check — a living map of your vocabulary.

  • Level as size. Easy words large, hard words small — your level reads in the grain of the cloud.
  • State as a badge. Bookmark for studying, check for known. One tap opens meaning and examples.
