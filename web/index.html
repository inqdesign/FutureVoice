How it works

Sixty seconds of your voice.
A lifetime of conversations.

01

Read for one minute

One short script, in a quiet room, on the built-in mic. That's the only setup Future Voice ever asks of you.

02

Meet your fluent self

We build a voice that is unmistakably yours — same timbre, same warmth — and give it the fluency you're working toward.

03

Talk, every day

Phone-call conversations about your actual life — work, kids, the weather that turned. Not flashcard sentences about airports.

Inside the app

Designed like a phone call,
not a chat app.

Talk

Pick a topic. Start talking.

Zero-tap conversations with live transcription and natural turn-taking — it waits when you're searching for a word, and answers when you're done. After each turn, a quiet suggestion shows how a fluent speaker would have put it.

No chat bubbles. No typing. Just your voice, twice.

Practice

Your mistakes become your curriculum.

Every line you fumbled comes back as a spaced-repetition drill — say it out loud first, then reveal how your fluent self said it. Shadow practice plays the line in your own voice, word by word, karaoke-style, and scores how closely you matched it.

Active recall, not multiple choice.

Why your own voice

Fluency stops feeling impossible the moment you hear it in your own voice.

Every language app shows you what fluent looks like. Future Voice lets you hear what your fluent sounds like — same timbre, same warmth, none of the hesitation. It's not a stranger to imitate. It's proof, in the one voice you can't dismiss.

Your practice stays on your device

Sessions, drills, and progress live on your iPhone — not on our servers.

Your clone, your call

Re-record or delete your voice clone anytime. Replacing it permanently deletes the old one.

One recording, ever

Sixty seconds at setup is all we ask for. The app never records you outside a conversation you started.
