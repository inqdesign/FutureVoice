The idea

Why it has to be
your voice.

01

A target you can actually hit

A native speaker's pitch, tone, and pace were never yours — you can imitate them forever and never arrive. Your fluent clone keeps your timbre, so the only distance left is fluency itself.

02

The gap becomes the lesson

Your sentence, then the fluent version — in the identical voice. Everything you hear in the difference is exactly what to fix: pronunciation, rhythm, phrasing. Nothing else to filter out.

03

Proof, not comparison

A fluent stranger proves it's possible for them. Your own fluent voice proves it's possible for you — it turns "someday" into a sound you've already heard.

The method

Speak it. Hear it. Make it stick.

A language sticks when you produce it yourself, hear it used in your own world, and drill it until it's automatic. That's the whole app: three tabs, one closed loop — all of it in your voice.

1Talk · Output

Speaking practice that writes your curriculum.

You only learn to speak by speaking — and the exact places you stall are a syllabus no textbook can see. Talk makes you produce, then catches what to fix.

  • Any topic, anytime. Free talk, a situation you choose, or news matched to your interests — start speaking practice on whatever you want to say. Every conversation is saved to your record.
  • Every turn, a fluent rewrite. Your sentence comes back the way your fluent self would say it — same meaning, better English.
  • Sessions end in a scorecard. Vocabulary, grammar, expressiveness, fluency, pronunciation — and every fumbled line becomes a drill card automatically.

2Watch · Input

You can't say what you've never heard.

The scariest part of speaking a foreign language is the blank — not knowing what to say next. In Watch, you see your fluent self carry that exact conversation. It feels like you talking: "so that's how I'd keep it going, that's how I'd put it."

  • Partners you created yourself. A colleague, your landlord, the pediatrician — tell it who they are and how you know each other, and it carries the conversation with real context: it understands both you and them.
  • Live every situation in advance. The negotiation, the small talk, the awkward ask — pick one, a full dialogue is generated, and every one of your lines flows out in your fluent voice.
  • Then shadow it into yours. Any line that sticks in your ear — one tap on "Shadow this" and you're speaking it until it's really yours.

3Practice · Imprint

Shadow it until it's automatic.

Understanding a phrase isn't owning it — your mouth needs the reps. Shadowing imprints pronunciation, rhythm, and intonation in one motion, and here the target is a voice you can actually match: yours.

  • Sync shadowing. The fluent line plays while you speak along, karaoke words pacing you — afterward, every word shows whether you nailed it, swapped it, or skipped it.
  • Loop the hard part. Tap the words that trip you to loop just that phrase, slow it to 0.5×, repeat — with a match score and coaching on pronunciation and pacing.
  • Spaced repetition. Your fumbled sentences return as speak-aloud drills right before you'd forget them.
