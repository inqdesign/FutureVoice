0160-second cloneOne short recording at setup builds the voice you'll have when you're fluent.
02Fluent rewritesMid-conversation, your sentence comes back the way your fluent self would say it.

04People from your lifeDescribe them out loud — they become personas that talk like the real person.
05Scenario dialoguesWatch your fluent self handle the situation, and shadow any line on the spot.
06Speak-aloud reviewFumbled lines return as drills right before you'd forget — answered out loud, never multiple choice.
