What nawana is

nawana clones your voice once and lets you practise a language by talking with a fluent version of yourself. The person on the other end of every call is an AI. It sounds like you and is often right, but it is a model, not a teacher: it can misunderstand you, correct something that wasn't wrong, or say something untrue with confidence. Use your judgement, especially before repeating a "correction" in a job interview.

It's a small independent product. Features change, sometimes get removed, and the AI behind them is swapped for better ones as they appear. We'll tell you when a change matters.
